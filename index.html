About the Index

A fan-made map of a fast-growing scene

DeshVTuber Hub is an independent, static directory built to help viewers find Indian VTubers across languages, platforms and agencies. Every profile is curated by hand — no scrapers, no auto-generated bios, no ranking algorithm deciding who gets seen.

Are you a VTuber or agency and want to be listed or corrected? Reach out through the links in the footer below.
